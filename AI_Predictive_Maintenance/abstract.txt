The continuous growth in global energy consumption, driven by population expansion, industrial
development, and urbanization, has intensified the need for reliable, efficient, and sustainable
energy systems. The environmental consequences of fossil fuel dependency, including increased
carbon emissions and climate change, have accelerated the global transition toward renewable
nergy sources. Among these sources, solar energy has emerged as a leading solution due to its
wide availability, economic viability, and minimal environmental impact. Nevertheless, the rapid
deployment of solar energy systems has introduced significant operational and maintenance
challenges, particularly related to the reliability of electrical motors that support critical functions
uch as solar tracking, cooling systems, pumping units, and auxiliary equipment.

Conventional maintenance strategies for electrical motors, including corrective and preventive
maintenance, are limited in their ability to detect early-stage faults and accurately predict
quipment failures. These traditional approaches often result in unexpected breakdowns, excessive
lowntime, reduced energy production, and increased operational and maintenance costs. In large-
cale solar power plants, motor failures can compromise system availability, reduce power
generation efficiency, and negatively impact the economic performance of renewable energy
projects.

This study presents an Artificial Intelligence (AI)-based predictive maintenance approach for
lectrical motors using advanced machine learning techniques. The proposed framework integrates
eal-time condition monitoring through multiple sensors measuring vibration, temperature,
current, and acoustic signals. Extracted features from these data streams are analyzed using
machine learning models to detect abnormal behavior, classify fault types, and predict the
emaining useful life of electrical motors.
